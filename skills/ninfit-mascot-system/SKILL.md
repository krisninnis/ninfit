# NinFit Mascot System

## Purpose

Use this skill for mascot domain design, progression, art behaviour, personality, collection, Legacy, cosmetics, conditions, interactions, memories, and Champion systems.

The mascot is an emotional reinforcement layer for real fitness progress.

It must support the fitness product, not compete with it.

## Core mascot rules

- Permanent mascot progress is never removed because of inactivity.
- Mascots never die, starve, or become sad because the user missed exercise.
- Do not shame users.
- Do not call a mascot “fat”.
- Do not use body weight or BMI as mascot progression criteria.
- Rarity and cosmetics never affect capability.
- No pay-to-win.
- No paid random eggs or loot boxes.
- Planned rest never counts as failure.
- Sustainable activity beats overtraining.

## Permanent stages

Stored internal IDs remain:

- `starter`
- `growing`
- `capable`
- `advanced`
- `elite`

Do not rename persisted IDs without an explicitly authorised migration.

Product labels are:

1. Starter
2. Growing
3. Active
4. Athletic
5. Champion

Change intensity:
- Starter → Growing: subtle
- Growing → Active: moderate
- Active → Athletic: stronger
- Athletic → Champion: dramatic

Progress should communicate:
- capability
- energy
- posture
- confidence
- learned activity skills
- gear/accessories

Do not communicate “thin = fit”.

## Permanent vs temporary state

Permanent appearance/progression and temporary condition must remain mechanically separate.

Temporary condition vocabulary currently includes:

- `energised`
- `normal`
- `resting`
- `slouch`
- `max_chill`

Conditions are:
- derived
- reversible
- adaptive to the user’s programme
- never persisted as a judgement about the person

Long inactivity may alter:
- posture
- energy
- clothing
- environment
- comedy props

Examples:
- hoodie
- joggers
- slippers
- beanbag
- blanket
- remote
- takeaway carton
- snack bag

## Starter species

Current intended five launch starters:

- Tortoise
- Bear
- Fox
- Otter
- Wolf

Starter art direction:
- recognisable real-animal-based creatures
- expressive
- premium creature-collecting-game finish
- slightly exaggerated
- strong silhouettes
- soft cel-shaded / premium mobile rendering
- not photorealistic
- not chibi
- young and capable, not helpless

Do not copy protected franchise designs.

## Starter selection

Future starter selection is:

**CURATED RANDOM**

Onboarding may create hidden weighting from:
- fitness path
- preferences
- personality input

Before hatch:
- no species name
- no silhouette
- no species-dependent clue

After first reveal, user gets one subtle one-time:
- “Not for me”

No paid rerolls.

## Personality

Species provides a base personality.

Possible tendencies:
- calm
- playful
- determined
- adventurous

Onboarding may slightly shape the individual.

Personality follows:

**Nature + Shared History**

A mascot begins with a stable core personality and develops secondary quirks through shared experiences.

Inactivity must never make the core personality “worse”.

## Shared Journey Bond

Bond grows through meaningful shared experiences such as:
- completing planned activities
- planned recovery/rest
- PBs
- milestones
- stage progression
- returning after absence
- trophies
- Secret Prestige
- Champion/evolution events

Bond never decays.

Avoid grindable friendship-point systems.

Preferred concept:
- New Companion
- Getting to Know You
- Training Partners
- Trusted Partners
- Lifelong Partners

Exact naming may evolve later.

## Favourite activities

Favourites should develop through shared history.

Species/personality may create tendencies, but actual favourites emerge from the user’s journey.

Examples:
- Favourite: Yoga
- Also loves: Walking
- Signature activity: Cycling

Champion may permanently record favourites for that chapter.

## Living Memory Book

Record a small curated set of meaningful memories.

Good memories:
- first activity
- PB
- Champion
- return after absence
- Legacy interactions
- funny mascot events
- first seasonal event
- major milestones

Do not record negative “failure” memories such as missed-workout counts.

Allow occasional user-saved memories later.

## Champion Ceremony

Champion should be a major emotional payoff.

Preferred ceremony:
- user finishes qualifying real activity
- special moment becomes available
- user explicitly starts ceremony
- recap shared journey
- show meaningful milestones
- reveal Champion form
- award permanent Champion record/relic
- Legacy Champions may attend
- user gets time with Champion before evolution

Do not immediately replace the Champion.

## Champion Relic

Each Champion creates a permanent personalised relic.

Relic may reflect:
- species
- dominant journey/activity
- major accomplishments
- generation
- bond
- Secret Prestige

Champion Relics cannot be bought.

## Living Legacy

Old Champions are never deleted.

After passing the Legacy forward:
- old Champion moves into collection
- keeps passport, memories, bond, appearance, achievements
- may naturally visit meaningful moments
- may be deliberately invited back

Legacy mascots may interact through a lightweight Living Family system.

Avoid a full mascot-to-mascot social simulator.

## Legacy Memories

Occasional personality-driven interactions between generations may become rare memories.

Predecessor/successor relationships should be recognised.

## Evolution

After Champion, eventual evolution flow:

1. Champion creates/lays next Opal Egg.
2. Egg cracks.
3. New mascot hatches.
4. Old and new interact.
5. Brief Opal energy connection/merge.
6. New mascot becomes active.
7. Old Champion moves permanently into collection.

Old mascot is never deleted.

Evolution is hybrid branching.

Future eligibility may consider:
- fitness path
- activity history
- personalised programme engagement

User chooses among eligible options.

## Legacy inheritance

New mascot inherits only cosmetic/personality history:

1. one subtle visual legacy token
2. one personality tendency
3. one legacy badge

No gameplay power inheritance.

## Secret Prestige

Secret Prestige is achievement-only.

It may recognise:
- sustained personal improvement
- PB patterns
- personalised consistency
- meaningful long-term engagement

Do not make absolute athletic performance the sole requirement.

Secret Prestige:
- cannot be purchased
- is not normal rarity
- does not affect capability
- should feel mysterious
- may unlock species-specific prestige features
- may escalate through multiple discoveries

Possible features:
- Opal marks
- capes
- wings
- auras
- species-specific traits
- Legacy effects

Before first unlock, NinFit may only hint that rare traits exist.

After first unlock, a Secret Prestige passport section may appear without revealing exact formulas.

Cheat codes must never grant earned Secret Prestige.

## Rarity

Cosmetic variant rarity may include:
- Normal
- Uncommon
- Rare
- Epic
- Legendary

Rarity is cosmetic only.

Use curated surprise plus protection from terrible luck.

Legendary should not be simple 0.1% RNG.

Long-term Legacy participation may create eligibility.

Rarity and prestige remain separate systems.

## Old-school secrets and cheats

NinFit may include:
- secret phrases
- interaction sequences
- developer Easter eggs

Cheats may affect:
- appearance
- animations
- sounds
- harmless environments
- comedy

Cheats must never alter or falsify:
- fitness history
- PBs
- XP
- levels
- trophies
- evolution eligibility
- Secret Prestige
- health data

Rule:

**Cheats can change fun. They can never fake achievement.**

## Smart Wardrobe

User controls the mascot’s core personal look.

NinFit may temporarily add contextual gear:
- helmet
- yoga mat/accessory
- trainers
- goggles
- recovery clothing
- seasonal accessories

After context ends, return to the user-selected look.

Secret Prestige should not be silently hidden by automatic gear.

## Activity-aware behaviour

Mascots may visually participate in:
- yoga
- walking
- running
- cycling
- strength
- mobility/stretching
- swimming
- recovery/rest

Activity mirroring must never imply the user should attempt an advanced pose.

Use activity groups rather than bespoke art for every small activity.

## Living Trophy Room

The Trophy Room may:
- grow naturally through achievements
- allow optional customisation
- house Legacy mascots
- display Champion Relics
- contain rare discoveries and Easter eggs

It should feel like a personal clubhouse built by years of showing up.

## Living seasons

Seasonal content may change:
- habitat
- Trophy Room
- animations
- optional challenges
- decorations

Avoid FOMO.

Missing a seasonal event is not failure.

Most seasonal content may return.

Dated items should represent memories, not incomplete collections.

## Programme-aware mascot quests

Mascot quests may reinforce only the safe programme already determined appropriate.

The game may encourage the programme.

It may not escalate the programme.

No mascot should encourage harder, longer, or more frequent activity solely for game rewards.
