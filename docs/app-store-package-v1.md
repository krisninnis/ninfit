## 5. Long description — launch draft

**Move more. Notice your progress. Build a journey that feels like yours.**

NinFit is a calm, fitness-first companion designed to make everyday movement feel meaningful without turning exercise into pressure.

Plan simple activity, record your progress, follow your weekly rhythm and build a lasting history of what you actually did. As NinFit grows, real walks and workouts become part of a Living Journey — with maps, achievements, personal milestones and a mascot that responds to genuine progress rather than demanding attention.

### Built around real progress

NinFit is designed around the things you genuinely do. Your movement, consistency and personal milestones shape your journey.

- Track daily activity and planned movement.
- Review your week and progress over time.
- Record personal measurements and fitness trends.
- Build consistency without punishment for missed days.
- Celebrate earned milestones and personal bests.
- Keep game and reward systems tied to genuine fitness evidence.

### Living Journeys

NinFit is being built to turn recorded walks and workouts into meaningful Journeys. A Journey can combine trustworthy evidence from your phone and, where you choose to connect them, compatible health and wearable services.

NinFit is designed so one real-world activity stays one Journey even when more than one device reports it. Imported records keep their source information rather than silently pretending they came from somewhere else.

### Private by default

Fitness and location data deserve careful handling.

NinFit is designed around privacy-first principles, including private-by-default Journeys, separate handling for precise GPS routes, clear source information and user control over deletion and future sharing.

Some features may remain on-device unless you explicitly choose future cloud or sharing capabilities.

### A game that supports fitness — not the other way around

NinFit uses a living mascot and reward layer as emotional reinforcement for genuine progress. There are no punishment mechanics for taking a rest day, and the product direction excludes pay-to-win fitness achievements or paid fabrication of activity, PBs, trophies or prestige.

### Built to grow with you

NinFit's direction includes:

- GPS Journey recording and route maps;
- wearable and health-platform integration;
- earned achievements and personal milestones;
- route privacy controls;
- richer progress insights;
- a living mascot, trophy room and long-term journey history.

Availability varies by release. Store copy must never imply that a future or experimental feature is already included in the submitted build.

### Important

NinFit is intended for fitness and wellbeing. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent medical conditions. If you have health concerns or are unsure whether an activity is appropriate for you, seek appropriate professional advice.

## 16. Privacy-summary draft

This section is planning copy and must be reconciled against actual implemented data flows and current store disclosure forms before submission.

### Plain-language summary

NinFit is designed to collect only the information needed for the features you use. Fitness history and precise location are treated differently, and Journeys are intended to be private by default. Connected wearable information should retain its source, and importing the same activity through multiple services should not create duplicate achievements.

Where data can remain on your device, NinFit should make that clear. Any future cloud sync, analytics, wearable connection or sharing feature must be reflected in the final privacy notice and the relevant store privacy/data-safety declarations.

## 23. Pricing language guardrail

No pricing model is locked by this file.

If subscriptions or purchases are introduced later:

- price and renewal wording must match the store product exactly;
- free-trial wording must state material conditions clearly;
- cancellation route must be understandable;
- no paid feature may fabricate fitness evidence or achievement truth;
- avoid manipulative countdowns or false scarcity;
- distinguish premium convenience/content from genuine earned fitness progress.
